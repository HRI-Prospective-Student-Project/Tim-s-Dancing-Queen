What is Computer Science at F&M?

At F&M, computer science is one of the most versatile fields of study, connecting technology with real-world impact. The program emphasizes both the mathematical foundations of computer science and strong programming skills.

  • • Strong foundations in theory and programming.
  • • Understand how computers process information.
  • • Explore AI and machine learning.
